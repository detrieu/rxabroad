# Legal classes and practice, by country

Drafted 2026-10-19 for the clinician (Pro) side. Not shown in the app. Each note compares the country with the US
(over the counter or prescription) and cites its source. Practice findings come from published studies, mostly on
antibiotics, and several are small or old: treat them as background for advising a patient, not as consumer advice.

Label: **As written** (classes differ from the US, no evidence the rules are loosely applied), **Exceptions in law**,
**Often loose** (studies find prescription medicines commonly sold without one).

| Country | Label | Note | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| US | Baseline | Two classes: over the counter or prescription. Pseudoephedrine needs no prescription but is kept behind the counter, with photo ID and a monthly limit. | [FDA, CMEA](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/postmarket-drug-safety-information-patients-and-providers/legal-requirements-sale-and-purchase-drug-products-containing-pseudoephedrine-ephedrine-and) |
| GB | As written | Three classes: general sale (any shop), pharmacy (ask at the counter) and prescription only. The same medicine can be general sale in a small pack and prescription only in a larger one. | [gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/medicines-reclassify-your-product) |
| CA | As written | Four tiers: Schedule I needs a prescription, Schedule II is behind the counter with the pharmacist, Schedule III is on pharmacy shelves, unscheduled sells anywhere. Strength and pack size can change the tier. | [NAPRA](https://www.napra.ca/national-drug-schedules/what-are-the-national-drug-schedules/) |
| DE | As written | Three classes: prescription, pharmacy only (most non-prescription medicines, sold at an Apotheke counter) and free sale (drugstores, supermarkets). | [gesund.bund.de](https://gesund.bund.de/arzneimittel-mit-und-ohne-rezept) |
| FR | As written | All medicines are sold in pharmacies only. Some non-prescription medicines are on open shelves (an ANSM list), the rest behind the counter. | [ANSM](https://ansm.sante.fr/documents/reference/medicaments-en-acces-direct) |
| IT | Exceptions in law | Four classes: OTC (open shelves, also parafarmacie and supermarkets), SOP (no prescription, behind the counter), RR (repeatable prescription), RNR (new prescription each time). A pharmacist may supply a prescription medicine without one for ongoing treatment of a chronic condition, to avoid interrupting a course, or after hospital discharge, with proof such as an old prescription or packaging. | [DM 31 March 2008](https://regione.lazio.it/sites/default/files/documentazione-farmaci/DM_31_marzo_2008.1215007948.pdf), [AIFA](https://www.aifa.gov.it/-/liberalizzazioni-facciamo-parlare-i-numeri) |
| ES | As written | Prescription or non-prescription; antibiotics need a prescription. A 2016-17 simulated-patient study found about 1 in 5 pharmacies in northwest Spain supplied antibiotics without one under pressure. | [JAC 2019](https://academic.oup.com/jac/article/74/2/511/5161208) |
| PT | As written | Three classes: prescription (MSRM), pharmacy only without prescription (MNSRM-EF), and non-prescription (MNSRM) that registered shops such as parafarmácias may also sell. | [INFARMED](https://www.infarmed.pt/documents/15786/2219894/Novo+ciclo+10+anos+ap%C3%B3s+a+liberta%C3%A7%C3%A3o+do+mercado+em+Portugal/1f88cdfa-90e3-47b5-9c32-9199406cdf82) |
| SG | As written | Three classes: prescription only, pharmacy only (the pharmacist may record name and ID) and general sale. Loratadine (Carin) moved to general sale in 2025. | [HSA](https://www.hsa.gov.sg/announcements/therapeutic-products-regulation/), [HSA reclassified list](https://www.hsa.gov.sg/therapeutic-products/registration-of-therapeutic-products/variation-application/reclassification/list-of-reclassified-medicines/) |
| JP | As written | Prescription medicines need a prescription; a 2025 law change bans selling them without one (reibai) except in narrow emergencies. Non-prescription medicines come in classes; guidance-required and Class 1 are sold only by a pharmacist. | [Yakuji Nippo](https://www.yakuji.co.jp/entry138258.html), [Shiga prefecture](https://www.pref.shiga.lg.jp/ippan/kenkouiryouhukushi/yakuzi/350252.html) |
| MX | Often loose | Antibiotic prescriptions have been retained by the pharmacy since 2010, but enforcement is weak; a border study found 65% of antibiotics sold without a prescription. Many pharmacies have an adjoining doctor's office. | [PMC border study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3606934), [INSP](https://www.insp.mx/images/stories/Lineas/medicamentos/doc/131115_antibiotics.pdf) |
| TR | Often loose | No legal over-the-counter class. Most medicines are on a normal prescription; narcotics and psychotropics on red or green. An Istanbul study (2003-04 data) found 41% of medicines sold without a prescription. | [Istanbul study](https://avesis.medipol.edu.tr/yayin/9e2f5c11-5a59-441d-86ee-1f271db19cde/nonprescription-medication-purchases-and-the-role-of-pharmacists-as-healthcare-workers-in-self-medication-in-istanbul) |
| IN | Often loose | Schedule H, H1 and X need a prescription; H1 sales must be recorded, X is strictest. Studies find Schedule H medicines often sold without a prescription. | [PMC 2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8092171), [PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4525524) |
| VN | Often loose | Non-prescription list or prescription. In a national simulated-patient study, pharmacies supplied antibiotics to 84% of adults who asked directly and 92% presenting with respiratory symptoms. | [Univ. of Sydney](https://www.sydney.edu.au/sydney-southeast-asia-centre/latest-news/news/2022/05/05/tracking-inappropriate-antibiotic-supply.html) |
| ET | Often loose | Only medicines on the EFDA OTC list may be sold without a prescription, often in set forms and pack sizes. An Addis Ababa simulated-patient study (2015) found most prescription medicines, antibiotics included, supplied without one. | [EFDA OTC list](https://www.efda.gov.et/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/OTC-medicines-list.pdf), [PMC 2016](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4948721/) |
| MA | Often loose | Studies find antibiotics easy to buy without a prescription. Legal classes not checked against an official source. | [PMC 2024](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11165378/) |

Gaps: no sources found on enforcement in DE, FR, JP or SG (so no claim either way); Japan's acceptance of foreign
prescriptions not sourced; Italy's placement could change if evidence of routine counter sales turns up.
